# LocalLens

Your community. Your news.

Developed by FrogDog Studios.

## About LocalLens

LocalLens is an Android news aggregation application that helps users discover local news, weather, and community information based on their selected location.

LocalLens brings together news from third-party publishers and services in one convenient application.

## News sources and attribution

LocalLens is a news aggregator and is not the original publisher of third-party news articles.

Original news content is attributed to its respective publishers. Questions regarding original reporting or editorial corrections should be directed to the relevant publisher.
