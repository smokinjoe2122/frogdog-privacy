# LocalLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

FrogDog ("we" or "us") provides the LocalLens Android app. This policy explains how LocalLens handles information when you use the app.

## Location

LocalLens uses location to show local news, weather, and location-related content. You can enter a city instead of granting device location access. If you grant permission, the app can access your device's location; depending on your Android settings and permission choice, this may be approximate or precise. You can change or revoke location permission in your device settings.

The app may send a city name or location coordinates to third-party services to find news, determine a location, retrieve weather, or display map-related content. For example, Google News may receive a search containing a city name, and Open-Meteo may receive coordinates for weather forecasts. Android's location and geocoding services may also process location information under their own privacy practices.

## Information stored on your device

LocalLens stores information such as your selected location, settings, and saved stories on your device so the app can remember your choices. This information remains until you change or remove it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Your Android device's backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up or restored.

## Third-party services

LocalLens connects to third-party services to provide content, including Google News, news publishers, Open-Meteo for weather and location searches, OpenStreetMap services for location and map information, and Wikimedia Commons for images. The app may also load images, videos, and articles from publishers or their content providers.

When your device connects to these services, they may receive your IP address, request details, and other technical information. Depending on the feature, a request may include a city, search term, or coordinates. Third parties handle information under their own privacy policies, including when you open their websites. FrogDog does not control their practices.

## App purchases

If you purchase LocalLens through Google Play, Google processes your payment and purchase information according to its own privacy policy. FrogDog does not receive your full payment card details through the app.

## Contact and deletion

To remove data stored by LocalLens on your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data held by third parties or in device backups is subject to those services' settings and policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LocalLens or its data practices change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the date above.
